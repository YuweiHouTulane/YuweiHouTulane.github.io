About

I'm a researcher at Tulane University working at the intersection of computational genomics and skeletal biology. My work uses single-cell and spatial transcriptomics alongside human genetics to ask how cell populations in bone and marrow change with age and disease.

Before Tulane I trained in [field] at [institution]. I'm currently interested in [specific problem], and I'm always glad to talk with people working on related questions.

Research interests

  • Single-cell & spatial transcriptomics Cell-type deconvolution and niche inference in tissue sections; methods for resolving sparse, high-resolution spatial data.
  • Human genetics & causal inference Integrating GWAS summary statistics with expression data to move from association to mechanism.
  • Skeletal & marrow biology Bone marrow adipose tissue, osteoporosis, and the cellular basis of age-related bone loss.
